## The patterns

1. **Blend length.** Both 64-bar blends were halved (T6 64 -> 31, T9 64 -> 34). The mid-length
   ones that leaned on a long incoming intro or an intro trim were cut hard (T4 32 -> 23, T5 36 ->
   20, T7 30 -> 15). Long blends survived only where the outgoing's OWN last bars loop under the
   incoming's intro and drop (T1 50, T3 48, T8 34). Sam's overlaps: 15-50, median 31.
2. **No intro front-trims.** Both were reversed (Pat Premier 16 bars, Coldabank 9 bars). The whole
   intro plays; the outgoing runs out under it, and the incoming's drop arrives on its own (9 bars
   after Zaro stops at T4, 17 bars after Arielle stops at T7).
3. **Entry on the outgoing's late landmark, 8-16 bars before the swap.** The `last_bass_drop`
   hint twice (T6 bar 146, T7 bar 107), a kick dropout (T4 bar 116), the last drop's start (T5 bar
   64). The pipeline validates `last_bass_drop_sec` in Phase 1f.5 but only ever offers it as a
   SWAP anchor (under `--cue-signals hints`); Sam uses it as the ENTRY point.
4. **Swap.** Incoming's drop_1 on 8 of 11, unchanged. Kick dropout at T5, unchanged. Moved to an
   intro phrase point (Coldabank bar 8) on the outgoing's outro start at T7. No EQ swap at T4.
   Two nudges: onto the outgoing's 8-bar grid at T2 (135 -> 136), and to AFTER the outgoing's
   4-bar fill at T9 (120 -> 124).
5. **Outgoing tail.** Loops of the outgoing's own last 1-2 bars were kept, extended or added
   (Demarkus last bar x16, Zaro +7 bars, Coldabank x8). Loops built from other material were
   removed (Tommy Farrow's intro drums; Switch Disco's). Outros were cut (RUZE -4 bars, Switch
   Disco -10, Cevin Fisher -1). The outgoing ends 12-18 bars after the swap.
6. **Levels.** An outgoing that plays its own ending is not faded to zero: 0.69 (T6), 0.82 (T7),
   0.24 (T9, T10). Incoming sneak varies with the material: 1.0 (Pat Premier), 0.53 (Coldabank),
   0.24 (Zaro), 0.12 (Tommy Farrow), 0.15-0.2 elsewhere.

## What this says to the arranger

- `plan_fill_or_cut` step 2, the intro cut: 0 of 2 kept. On this evidence it should not fire when
  the outgoing can run out under the intro instead.
- Nothing in `align_engine` enters the incoming at an outgoing landmark this late. `sam_v1`'s
  `extend_incoming_entry` goes the other way (earlier, looped). The hint is already computed.
- The `named_landmark_64` lane: its two 64-bar uses here were both halved; the 48-52 bar ones
  were kept. The difference is what fills the extra bars, not the count.
- A swap must not land inside a fill (T9), and should snap to the outgoing's phrase grid when the
  detected boundary is an odd-bar section (T2). The BAR-MATH warning is the signal.
- `apply_automation`: when the outgoing ends before the incoming's drop, no bass kill is needed
  (T4); when the outgoing plays its own ending, the fade-to-zero envelope is wrong (T6, T7, T9,
  T10). This is the C2 question (`_outgoing_has_post_swap_content`) from the other side.
- Learner gap: `learn_from_correction.py` labelled 5 of the 11 (3 `bass_swap_moved`, 2
  `sneak_changed`). Entry landmarks, intro-trim reversals, loop removals and outro cuts are
  recorded only as overlap deltas and `arrangement_changed: true`. `analyze_correction_diff.py`
  captures the cues (`15.09.26 August Releases Mix Sam Tweaks.diff.json`, beside this note) but
  nothing consumes them. Burn list D12.
