**Pattern:** where I targeted the outro/last-bar, Sam targets the END OF A DROP —
he swaps while the outgoing still has energy and lets its own remaining material
(not a synthetic loop) carry the exit. Where I built long outgoing tails, he
shortened or cut. Net: the mix lost 256 beats (~2 min tighter).

## When extension IS needed, loop the INCOMING'S INTRO, not the outgoing's tail

T9 is the proof: he deleted Kideko's 32b×4 tail loop and instead **looped Demarkus'
64-beat intro ×2** (arr 5152-5280) to fill the run-up to the swap. The one loop he
ADDED anywhere is an incoming-intro loop. `align_engine.plan_fill_or_cut` already
has an incoming-intro-loop path — the correction says the PREFERENCE is inverted:
incoming-intro extension should outrank outgoing-tail extension.

## Endings are clip-cuts, not fades

Several corrected transitions show `outgoing_kill_beat: null` and non-zero
`outgoing_exit_level` (T1 exit level 1.0, T3 0.29, T5 0.27): he often doesn't ride
the Utility fade to zero or write an EQ kill — the outgoing clip simply ENDS at a
musical boundary. (Some of this is speed over polish, per his own caveat — but the
consistency across transitions says the shape is intentional: cut at the phrase,
don't taper.)
