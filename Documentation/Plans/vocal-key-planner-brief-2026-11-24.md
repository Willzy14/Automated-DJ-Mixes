# Build brief: vocal clash and key compatibility influence the blend planner (Sam approved 2026-11-24)

Written 2026-11-24 by Claude (Sonnet 5.5) for Sam. Builder: a Sonnet subagent. Reviewers: MiniMax plus an independent Opus subagent, then the lead's executing check. No em dashes anywhere (code, comments, output, docs, commit text). Python output ASCII only.

## Why

Sam asked, after the "what have we missed" review: "make vocal clash and key actually influence the blend." Today neither does. `Source/propose_arrangement.py::_finalize_vocal_clash` finds overlapping vocal passages inside each transition but its docstring says "Report-only ... never touches anything that affects what gets built". `Source/automated_dj_mixes/sequencer.py` uses Camelot keys to ORDER the playlist, but the blend planner (`Source/phrase_blend.py`, driven by `Tools/plan_mix_decisions.py`) never sees vocals or keys: its per-track content is only `has_kick`, `has_bass`, `has_music` per bar, built by `convert_track` from the stem envelope. So a blend can overlap two lead vocals, or two tonal passages in clashing keys, at no cost.

## The design (decided by the lead; challenge it only with a concrete hole)

Add TWO soft cost terms to the planner's score. Soft, never a ban: the planner already lists alternatives, so a clash that cannot be avoided must still be plannable and must be REPORTED on the plan.

1. **Vocal clash term.** Per-bar `has_vocal` for each track comes from the detector's `signals["vocal_regions"]` in `_Stem Analysis/SECTIONS_STEM_<track>.json` (entries are `[start_sec, end_sec, start_bar, end_bar]`, bars in the track's own bar numbers; see `align_engine.py` line ~701 for how they are read and `stem_detector.py` line ~1358 for how they are made). In `convert_track` add `content["has_vocal"]` as a per-bar boolean list on the same bar clock as `has_kick` and friends. A new field is OPTIONAL: when a track has no `has_vocal`, the planner behaves byte for byte as before (every existing test and the fixtures `Tests/Fixtures/planner_pair3.json`, the DFTD content files must give identical plans). The cost: for every bar of the OVERLAP window (from the incoming track's entry to the outgoing track's end-of-overlap, as the planner already counts bars for its content checks; read `content_check` and `_out_state` / `_in_state` to see how a bar of each track is mapped) in which BOTH tracks have a vocal, add `W_VOCAL_CLASH` per bar. Bars that play only one track's vocal cost nothing. Think about the bass-swap point: after the swap the outgoing track is mostly gone in the low end but its vocal still plays, so count the whole overlap, not only before the swap.
2. **Key clash term.** Key per track as a Camelot code (e.g. `8A`) or None. Where it comes from is for you to find and state: look at what the pipeline already carries (`ARRANGEMENT_REPORT.json` transitions have `harmonic_score` and `harmonic_type`, currently None on the Tech House run, so find out why it is None and where keys are read: `sequencer.key_to_camelot`, `mik_reader.py`, the ID3/Neon key, the Audio Analysis Toolkit at `C:\Users\Carillon\Wired Masters Dropbox\Sam Wills\0.1---GIT HUB---\Audio Analysis Toolkit`). Use the source that is already in the pipeline; do NOT build a new key detector. If no key is available for a track the term is zero for that pair and the plan says "key unknown". The cost: the Camelot distance of the pair (same number any letter, or +-1 same letter, or relative major/minor = compatible, cost 0; each further step costs more, capped) times the number of overlap bars in which BOTH tracks have `has_music` (tonal content). Overlap bars that are only kick and bass cannot clash tonally, so they cost nothing. A big clash must cost a little less than a blend that Sam's cost rules already reject, never more than the cost of a forbidden outro cut (`W_CUT` 12).
3. **Weights come from Sam's own blends, not from taste.** Before choosing `W_VOCAL_CLASH` and the key weight, MEASURE how often Sam's own placements overlap vocals and clash keys: (a) the 7 DFTD answer-key blends (`Documentation/Golden Sections/Answer Key 2026-11-20 DFTD.json`, `Tools/score_blends.py`); (b) the 5 blends Sam changed and 3 he kept on the Tech House mix (`Tools/score_tweak.py`, `Source/tweak_replay.py`; data in `D:\Claude Workspace\Automated DJ Mixes\Projects\21.11.26 Tech House 2026 V2\`). If Sam himself often overlaps vocals for a few bars, a heavy penalty is wrong; set the weights so that the clash bars Sam actually leaves in (the median and the maximum) cost less than the cost of moving the swap by a phrase (8 bars, about 2.0). Print the table of clash bars and key distance for each of Sam's blends and the planner's pick for each, in your report.
4. **The plan reports it.** `BlendPlan` gets the fields `vocal_clash_bars` and `key_clash` (the numbers) and the terms appear in `BlendPlan.terms`; `Tools/plan_mix_decisions.py` prints a line per blend ("vocals overlap 6 bars", "key 8A to 3B clash over 12 tonal bars" or "key unknown"). The existing report-only `_finalize_vocal_clash` in `propose_arrangement.py` stays untouched.
5. **Do not regress.** `Tools/score_blends.py` on the DFTD answer key and `Tools/score_tweak.py` on the Tech House tweak must be run before and after: the number of blends where the planner's top pick equals Sam's, and where his is within the listed alternatives, must not go down (if it does, say so and show which blend and why; do not tune to hide it). Plans for blends with no vocal or no key data must be identical to before.

## Tests (each must fail on the old code)

- A pair where the old top plan overlaps vocals for N bars and an alternative of equal other cost does not: the new top pick is the alternative. A pair where every option clashes: plan still produced, `vocal_clash_bars` reported, cost term present.
- Key: compatible pair costs 0; far pair over tonal overlap costs more; the same far pair over a kick-and-bass-only overlap costs 0; unknown key costs 0 and says so.
- Optional-field behaviour: tracks without `has_vocal` give a plan equal to the old one (use the existing fixtures).
- `convert_track` builds `has_vocal` on the right bar clock (a vocal region at bars 8 to 16 sets exactly those bars, on a track with a nonzero `lead_in_bars`).
- Mutants to kill and prove: clash counted only before the swap; key term applied to non-tonal bars; the vocal term applied when only one track has a vocal; `has_vocal` read on the wrong track; weights large enough to veto (W above `W_CUT`).
