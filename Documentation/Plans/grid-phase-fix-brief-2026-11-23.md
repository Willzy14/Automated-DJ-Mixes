## Why

The kick alignment check (merged 2026-11-23, `Source/kick_alignment_check.py`, `Tools/check_kick_alignment.py`, pipeline hook; read its TOOLBOX entry and the file first) measures the lag between the two kick streams in every blend. On Sam's Tech House 2026 mix it found a flam in Get Right into No Panties and No Panties into Taste. Sam then got it tight with TWO manual moves, and said of the result: "this is as tight as I would get it manually":

1. Get Right's grid drifted: its kicks sat 2 ms early at the start and 6 ms early by the end against a straight grid. Fix: re-fit each of its warp markers to the measured per-beat kick offsets (smoothed running median: window plus or minus 48 beats, at least 8 kick beats, outliers over 15 ms dropped, then a 31-beat moving average; correction -2.0 ms at the start to -6.1 ms at the end).
2. No Panties has a slow-attack kick (small early click, main hit later), so one track-level slide had to balance its two blends: +5 ms on all its warp markers (SecTime plus 5 ms). Choosing the slide by the minimax of the cross-correlation CLICK lag across the track's blends matched Sam's ear (his first pick, 8.5 ms, was the same rule before the Get Right fix; after the fix the balance point fell to 5 ms).

Goal: the pipeline does both moves itself, after the final ALS exists, and shows the before and after with the kick check.

## The key property that makes the slide solvable

A slide of a track's markers by s ms (SecTime plus s ms, so its kicks land s ms EARLIER in the arrangement) changes blend lag (incoming minus outgoing, positive = incoming later) exactly additively: for the blend k between track k (outgoing) and track k+1 (incoming), lag_k = L_k + s_k - s_{k+1}, where L_k is the measured lag with no slide and s are the slides (markers later = positive). Measured proof: Get Right into No Panties click lag was 10.1 ms unslid, 4.1 at +6.4, 2.0 at +8.5, -1.5 at +12 (NP incoming, so lag falls 1 ms per ms of slide); No Panties into Taste (NP outgoing) rose from -5.7 by about the slide. So the lags can be computed from ONE measurement run; do not re-measure per candidate slide.

## What to build

1. `Source/grid_phase_fix.py` (library), `Tools/fix_grid_phase.py` (CLI), a hook in `Tools/run_mix_pipeline.sh`, tests. Work in the git worktree only (see Rules).
2. Library, read-only on inputs, text-patch only on the output ALS (follow the project's ALS rules in `Documentation/ABLETON_INTERACTION.md` and the `ableton-als-editing` skill: gzip, `splitlines(keepends=True)`, CRLF preserved, never write XML with ElementTree, change only the `SecTime` value of `<WarpMarker ... />` lines of the chosen tracks, keep float precision with `format(v, '.18g')`). Steps:
   a. Parse with the kick check library (reuse `read_als`, `find_transitions`, `source_position`, the window reader, kick-beat test and edge estimator from `Source/kick_alignment_check.py`; add small public helpers there only if needed, with tests). Read short windows only; never load a whole wav.
   b. DRIFT FIT per track: measure the leading-edge click offset (ms, kick minus grid, positive = kick late) on every kick beat of the source beats actually used by the track's clips; fit the correction as above (running median then moving average, flat at the ends, a track with fewer than 40 usable kick beats gets no correction and is reported `CANNOT_FIT`); a correction larger than `MAX_DRIFT_MS = 15` in magnitude is refused for that track and reported. Apply to each marker by its BeatTime (marker SecTime plus correction at that beat).
   c. After the virtual drift correction, measure the click lag of every blend (use the kick check's cross-correlation click median; blends that are CANNOT_JUDGE give no constraint and their tracks are not slid on their account). Lags shift additively by the drift corrections: compute them, do not re-run the audio read.
   d. SLIDE SOLVE: per track s_i with the model above. Linear program (scipy `linprog`, deterministic): minimise t + LAMBDA * sum_i |s_i| subject to |L_k + s_k - s_{k+1}| <= t for every judged blend, |s_i| <= `MAX_SLIDE_MS = 15`. LAMBDA small and fixed (start 0.02 per ms; justify in a comment and in a test). Only tracks that are in a judged blend may slide. If the worst judged |lag| after the drift fix is already under `WARN_MS` (from the kick check), do nothing (a good mix is never touched). Report the achieved t.
   e. Write the corrected ALS as a NEW file (never overwrite the input) and run the kick check on it: print a before and after table for every transition (edge, click, main, verdict) and the per-track drift range and slide. Refuse to keep the result (say so, exit code 4) if any judged blend's worst |click lag| got worse, or any blend became FAIL that was not FAIL before.
3. CLI: `python Tools/fix_grid_phase.py <ALS> --audio <dir> [--out <new ALS>] [--report <dir>]`. Exit codes: 0 ok or nothing to do, 2 could not run, 4 result refused. Writes a plain-text before/after report and the kick check pictures for the new file next to it.
4. Hook in `Tools/run_mix_pipeline.sh`, right after the existing kick alignment hook: runs the CLI on `$P/Output/Sections V$B.als`, writes `$P/Output/Sections V$B Phased.als` and `$P/Output/Grid Phase/`. Mode from `GRID_PHASE_MODE`: `suggest` (DEFAULT: writes the phased file and report, the final stays `Sections V$B.als`, a failure never stops the build) or `apply` (the phased file must pass `validate_als.py` and the MixPlan reconciliation, then it becomes the final; if either fails, keep the original and say so loudly). Print one line saying which mode and where the report is. Do not touch other phases. Do not edit `commands/mix.md` (frozen sync list; the lead does it).
