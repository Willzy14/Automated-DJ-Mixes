## The failure (real, on a never-seen mix)

Fresh mix project `D:\Claude Workspace\Automated DJ Mixes\Projects\24.11.26 Fresh Mix\` (track held back in `Held Back\`: `Harry Romero, Jose Nunez, Alex Alicea & Shawnee Taylor - I Wanna Thank You 24 Bit MASTER.wav`; its cache is in `_Stem Analysis\`). The beat-grid gate stopped: "stem grid is 127ms OFF its own kicks (>15ms)". A diagnosis job (read its files first: `D:\Claude Workspace\Automated DJ Mixes\romero-grid\`, `romero_kicks_vs_grid.png`, `detfix.py`, `verify.py`, measure1..7.py) found:

- The track is 126.00 BPM, four on the floor, kicks on 507 of 648 beats, four breakdowns. The grid was built at 120.29 BPM, so it slides about 22 ms further off every beat.
- Cause: `estimate_period` in `Audio Analysis Toolkit/src/audio_analysis/stem_grid.py` takes the most common single-beat gap. After `refine_to_click` the kick gaps on this track alternate (0.453, 0.504, 0.448, 0.500 s): on odd beats a loud high-frequency hit starts 20 to 30 ms before the kick body and the click snap locks onto it (even beats about +11 ms, odd about -10 ms against the 126.005 BPM lattice). The histogram mode is the 0.50 cluster, giving 0.4988 s, confidence 0.502.
- The sub-band (kick body) onsets are regular. A prototype patch (monkeypatched, not in any repo): when confidence is under 0.6, re-seed the period from 4-beat spans `(k[i+4]-k[i])/4` over runs of four consecutive single-beat gaps. Result: 126.000 BPM, confidence 1.00, `detect_beat_grid` flag '', `grid_vs_kick_ms` 11.42 (under the 15 ms gate), 672 beats, downbeat offset 0. The other six cached tracks of that mix were unchanged. Phase caveat (not yours to solve): that grid sits about 22 ms early against the kick bodies, a midpoint of the alternating clicks.
